About Andrew

I'm Andrew Duquet. I founded SearchLight Systems in 1995 and still run it myself. There are no junior recruiters and no hand-offs: the person you talk to on the first call is the person who represents you.

I have a BSEE, so I speak the language, and an MBA from the University of Texas at Austin, so I understand the business behind a hire. When I describe a role to an engineer, or an engineer's background to a hiring manager, I understand what the work actually involves. That's a big part of how SearchLight has made more than 2000 placements.

For engineers

  • Confidential. Your name and resume go to an employer only after you've agreed to that specific role.
  • Personal email only. I contact you at a personal address, never your work email.
  • No resume spam. I don't blast your background around. Each introduction is one role you've said yes to.
  • A wide market. Beyond my own clients, I can introduce you to hundreds of hiring companies with more than 1,000 open engineering roles.
  • Straight answers. I'll tell you what I know about the role, the team and the process, and say so when I don't know.

How I work

  1. Understand the role

    A conversation with the hiring manager about the work, the team and what a great hire looks like.

  2. Find the right people

    Targeted, personal outreach to engineers whose actual experience fits, not a keyword search.

  3. Talk before introducing

    I speak with each candidate first and introduce only the ones who fit and want the role.

  4. Stay in touch

    I stay your point of contact while the hiring team runs its interviews and decision.
