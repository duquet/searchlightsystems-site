About Andrew

I'm Andrew Duquet. I founded SearchLight Systems in 1995 and still run it myself. There are no junior recruiters and no hand-offs: the person you talk to on the first call is the person who manages the search through to an offer.

I have a BSEE, so I speak the language. When I describe a role to an engineer, or an engineer's background to a hiring manager, I understand what the work actually involves. That's a big part of how SearchLight has made more than 2000 placements.

For engineers

  • Confidential. Your name and resume go to an employer only after you've agreed to that specific role.
  • Personal email only. I contact you at a personal address, never your work email.
  • No resume spam. I don't blast your background around. Each introduction is one role you've said yes to.
  • Straight answers. I'll tell you what I know about the role, the team and the process, and say so when I don't know.

How I work

  1. Understand the role

    A conversation with the hiring manager about the work, the team and what a great hire looks like.

  2. Find the right people

    Targeted, personal outreach to engineers whose actual experience fits, not a keyword search.

  3. Talk before introducing

    I speak with each candidate first and introduce only the ones who fit and want the role.

  4. See it through

    I stay involved through interviews, offer and start date, keeping both sides informed.
